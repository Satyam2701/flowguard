# Sample Screen Inventory: Password Reset Designs

> **Illustrative data.** This text describes a fictional set of design frames so FlowGuard can be tried without image files. It is not a real design file.

Source: `password-reset-v3` (fictional Figma export, 7 frames, described in text)

## Frame 1: "Login"

- Email field, password field, "Sign in" button.
- Link: "Forgot password?" below the password field.

## Frame 2: "Reset - Enter email"

- Title: "Reset your password".
- Email field with label "Email address".
- Primary button: "Send reset link".
- Secondary link: "Back to sign in".

## Frame 3: "Reset - Enter email (error)"

- Same as Frame 2 with red outline on the email field.
- Inline message under the field: "Enter a valid email address".

## Frame 4: "Check your inbox"

- Title: "Check your email".
- Text: "If an account exists for that address, we sent a reset link. It expires in 30 minutes."
- Link: "Didn't get it? Resend".
- Link: "Back to sign in".

## Frame 5: "New password"

- Title: "Create a new password".
- Password field with an eye icon to show or hide.
- Helper text: "At least 12 characters, including a number."
- Primary button: "Update password".

## Frame 6: "New password (error)"

- Same as Frame 5.
- Inline error: "Password must be at least 12 characters."

## Frame 7: "Password updated"

- Check icon, title "Password updated".
- Text: "You can now sign in with your new password."
- Primary button: "Sign in".
